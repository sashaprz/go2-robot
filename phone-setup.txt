GO2 PHONE LINK - setup (iPhone 15 Pro)
======================================

What it does
  * The phone's MICROPHONE becomes the voice mic: say "ernest, follow me" into the air near the phone and the app hears it.
    (Before the phone connects, or if it drops out, the laptop mic is used automatically.)
  * The phone's MOTION SENSORS say whether you are walking or standing. If the camera loses you, the dog now WAITS while your phone says
    you have stopped, instead of giving up after 1-2 s. (It does not steer by the phone's compass/gyro: that didn't help in simulation.)

ONCE ONLY
  1. Make sure the laptop AND the iPhone are on the dog's Wi-Fi (the same network).
  2. On the laptop, right-click phone-firewall.bat > Run as administrator.
  3. Double-click go2.bat. The first launch takes ~10 s longer (it makes the certificates) and then opens phone_qr.html.
  4. On the iPhone open Safari and go to  http://<the laptop's address>:8080   (phone_qr.html shows it, step 1).
       - Tap "Download the certificate", Allow.
       - Settings > "Profile Downloaded" > Install (twice).
       - Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings > switch "go2-robot local CA" ON.
     (iPhones only give a web page the microphone and motion sensors over HTTPS, and only trust a certificate you install. This one can only
     vouch for local names and private addresses, never a real website.)

EVERY TIME
  1. Double-click go2.bat. It opens phone_qr.html. Scan the QR code with the iPhone camera (or type the link from phone_url.txt).
  2. Tap Start. Allow Microphone, then allow Motion & Orientation.
  3. Leave the page open and the screen ON (the page asks iOS to keep the screen awake; if the screen locks, Safari stops the stream and the
     app falls back to the laptop mic and the camera alone, and says so).
  4. The app's message area says "phone connected", then "the ear is now listening through the phone microphone".
     The top bar shows "phone mic <level>" while it is used.

The link contains a secret: don't share it. Delete .phone_token to change it (then re-scan).

Troubleshooting
  * The phone can't open the page: same Wi-Fi? phone-firewall.bat run? If the dog's Wi-Fi blocks phone-to-laptop traffic (some routers isolate
    clients) it can't work on that network: then use the laptop as a hotspot and put the phone AND the dog on... (the dog's Wi-Fi is the simplest).
  * "Your connection is not private": the certificate isn't installed/trusted yet (step 4 above), or the laptop's address changed since (just restart go2.bat: it makes a new one and the root stays trusted).
  * No app "phone connected": phone_server.log next to go2.bat says what it saw.
